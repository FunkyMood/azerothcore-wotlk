A classic-style WotLK realm built for adventuring alone or with friends. Helpful companions and flexible systems keep Azeroth ready for every journey.


|cffFFD700Features|r

- Playerbots: recruit adventuring companions to fill your party and explore the world together

- All Races All Classes: create any race/class combination, no restrictions

- AutoBalance: dungeons and raids scale their difficulty to your group size, playable solo or with friends

- Individual XP Rates: choose the leveling pace that suits your adventure

- Classic-leaning leveling: a more deliberate pace with meaningful combat and recovery

- No Profession Limit: learn all 11 primary professions on a single character

- Transmogrification: change your gear appearance while keeping its stats

- Auction House Bot: a populated market for buying and selling items
